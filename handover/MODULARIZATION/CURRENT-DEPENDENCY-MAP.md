- Approvals: one-shot `kriya launch-decision --launch-id --review-id --candidate-digest --decision [--kind runtime_command]` (`kriya_argv.ts:85-96`; `cli.py:209`).
- The one-shot `kriya traces --json ...`/`kriya evidence ...` argv grammar (`kriya_argv.ts`) still exists for the same operations.
