A CompletionStage returned by a decorated Retry (for example `Retry.decorateCompletionStage(retry, scheduler, supplier)`) never completes if a user-supplied predicate in the RetryConfig (such as the one passed to `retryOnException`) throws an exception or error while the retry decides what to do. The caller's future then hangs forever and nothing is logged. A returned CompletionStage must always complete: when such a predicate throws, complete the stage exceptionally with that failure. Behavior for predicates that do not throw must not change.
