Column-specific options are only validated when they are assigned as a whole: `table.align = "rice"` raises a `ValueError`, but `table.align["Field 1"] = "rice"` (assigning a single column's entry in the per-column dict) is silently accepted and later breaks table output, for example with a KeyError when producing HTML. Validate column-specific options when they are modified through the per-column dictionaries as well, raising the same `ValueError` as for the whole-option assignment, while valid single-column values such as `table.align["Population"] = "r"` keep working and affect only that column.
