# Fixture fidelity: what each fixture section is, and what the evidence checker says about it (2026-10-04)

Three kinds of fixture content exist under `ui/fixtures/` and `ui/kup/test/fixtures/`:

- **serializer-derived**: produced by Kriya's own writers and read back through the KUP adapter
  (`fixtures/serializer_*.py`, committed under `fixtures/serializer/`; `npm run fixtures:serializer:check` detects drift);
- **synthetic demonstration**: hand-written to exercise a panel or a limit (big payloads, every availability state, unknown
  fields), shaped like the serializer where a serializer shape exists, inventing no identifiers or links;
- **NEGATIVE CASE**: deliberately outside the contract, labelled in its goal/provenance, kept so the UI and the checker can be
  proven to refuse the inference.

## Expected limitation, kept on purpose

Production emits no attribution record and no evidence identifier, so there is no resolved evidence link anywhere in Kriya's
output today. The UI states "unresolved reference" for any recorded `evidence_ids`, the checker reports `EVC-REF-001`, and
neither is silenced by deleting the field from the one fixture that carries it (`run-negative-evidence-links`). When Kriya
gains a documented namespace, resolution belongs to a new contract version, not to a UI inference.
